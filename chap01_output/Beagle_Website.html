BEAGLES & RESEARCH IN UPSTATE NY

A page about the beagles bred for testing in western New York

Why This Matters

Beagles are small, gentle dogs that have long been used in biomedical research because of their size and calm temperament. In Upstate New York, one company has become the largest supplier of these dogs in the United States.

Marshall BioResources, located in North Rose (Wayne County) near Rochester, breeds thousands of beagles every year specifically for laboratories. Recent protests and a 200-mile march from Albany have brought national attention to the facility and the broader debate over animal testing.

5 Key Facts

  • Marshall BioResources is the nation’s largest breeder of dogs for research and is based in rural Upstate New York.
  • Beagles make up the majority of dogs used in U.S. laboratory testing because they are small and easy to handle.
  • In 2026, hundreds of activists marched more than 200 miles from Albany to the facility to protest animal testing.
  • Rescue groups have been working to rehome former research beagles so they can become family pets.
  • New federal bills have been introduced that would ban the breeding of dogs specifically for laboratory use.
